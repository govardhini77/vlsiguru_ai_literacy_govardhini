- [x] Distinguish AI, ML, DL, GenAI, and AI agents

## What I Learned

In Week 01, I learned the basic concepts of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI, and AI agents.

I also learned that not every automated system is AI. Traditional software can follow fixed rules, while many AI systems learn patterns from data or generate new outputs.

I learned the basic idea of how an LLM processes a prompt, works with tokens, and predicts the next token to generate a response. I also learned that an AI response can sound correct even when some information is wrong or unsupported.

## Main Takeaway

My main takeaway from Week 01 is that getting an answer from AI is not the same as verifying the answer.

For engineering work, I should understand the problem, inspect the AI output, check important information using reliable sources, test the result when possible, and make the final decision based on the verification.
